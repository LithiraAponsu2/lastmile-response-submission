# Prototype boundaries and controls

All operational exercises are fictional. LastMile is not an authorised emergency service, agency identity provider, dispatch/payment system or guarantee of safe evacuation, resource availability or real-world delivery.

- Normal account reads/writes are scoped on the server to actors, organisations and incidents. Demo role selection is an explicitly weaker fictional simulation and must use disposable demo-only records.
- Reports, attachments, photos and retrieved text remain untrusted inputs. Model interpretation is not evidence acceptance, proof of authenticity or permission to act.
- Models read/propose; operational plans require human review and server checks of actor scope, payload/hash, version, expiry and domain rules. Unknown counts remain unknown; source quotations and recorded quantities matter.
- Public intake applies code triage and has rules fallback for unavailable/capped/failed model reading. Neither prompts nor synthetic tests prove a zero false-closure or hallucination rate generally.
- Candidate duplicates require a human decision. Similar area/category alone must not imply identical beneficiaries or completion. Resident replies must remain visible to staff.
- Staff-recorded completion does not establish resident confirmation/reopening, receipt of physical aid or independent outcome verification.
- Maps, hospital lists, gauge references and standards are planning/reference material. They are not certified current hazard boundaries, safe routes, surveyed capacity, admission availability or a live authoritative register.
- English/Sinhala/Tamil interfaces and generated text require native-speaker review; photos and coordinator plans lack the same benchmark as saved public text intake.
- Legacy warning/subscriber caches and shared cross-incident resources retain one-writer constraints. Separate stores are not one distributed transaction.
- Public operational messages are recorded exercise artifacts. Legacy SMTP settings do not implement hub SMS/email dispatch or prove recipient understanding.

Use fictional local data for tests and reports. Keep real uploads, contacts, accounts, tokens, invitations and state outside Git. Preserve source/data notices and resolve pending rights before public release. [Third-party notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) · [AI evidence](EVIDENCE.md) · [Architecture](ARCHITECTURE.md).
